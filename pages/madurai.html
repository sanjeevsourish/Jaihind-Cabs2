TOP 5 SIGHTSEEING PLACES

Places You Should Not Miss.

Explore some of the most memorable places to visit in Madurai with Jaihind Cabs.

01
TEMPLE

Meenakshi Amman Temple

One of Madurai's most famous landmarks, celebrated for its magnificent Dravidian architecture, colourful towers and heritage.

02
HERITAGE

Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace

A magnificent historic palace known for its grand architecture, massive pillars, arches and royal heritage.

03
MUSEUM

Gandhi Memorial Museum

A historic museum offering visitors an opportunity to learn about India's freedom movement and Mahatma Gandhi's legacy.

04
TEMPLE

Alagar Koyil

A beautiful hill temple surrounded by scenic greenery, known for its traditional architecture and spiritual importance.

05
HILLSIDE

Pazhamudircholai

A peaceful Murugan temple located amid the greenery of the hills, offering a refreshing and spiritual experience.
