TOP 5 SIGHTSEEING PLACES

Places You Should Not Miss.

Explore some of the most memorable places to visit in Rameshwaram with Jaihind Cabs.

01
TEMPLE

Ramanathaswamy Temple

One of India's important pilgrimage landmarks, famous for its magnificent corridors, architecture and spiritual significance.

02
COASTAL

Dhanushkodi

A fascinating coastal destination at the eastern end of Rameshwaram, known for its dramatic sea views and historic atmosphere.

03
LANDMARK

Pamban Bridge

A famous engineering landmark connecting Rameshwaram Island with mainland Tamil Nadu, offering impressive coastal views.

04
BEACH

Agni Theertham

A revered seashore near Ramanathaswamy Temple, known for its peaceful coastal setting and religious importance.

05
MEMORIAL

APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial

A memorable landmark dedicated to India's former President and renowned scientist Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam.
