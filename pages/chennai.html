TOP 5 SIGHTSEEING PLACES

Places You Should Not Miss.

Explore some of the most memorable places to visit in Chennai with Jaihind Cabs.

01
BEACH

Marina Beach

One of Chennai's most famous landmarks, Marina Beach is known for its long coastline, sea breeze, beautiful views and lively atmosphere.

02
TEMPLE

Kapaleeshwarar Temple

A famous historic temple in Mylapore, celebrated for its Dravidian architecture, colourful gopuram and spiritual atmosphere.

03
HISTORIC

Fort St. George

A historic landmark of Chennai that offers visitors a glimpse into the city's colonial history, architecture and heritage.

04
CHURCH

Santhome Basilica

A beautiful historic basilica near the coast, known for its striking Gothic architecture and peaceful surroundings.

05
MUSEUM

Government Museum

One of Chennai's important cultural attractions, featuring collections covering art, archaeology, history, zoology and natural heritage.
